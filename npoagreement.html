Non Profit Organizing Documents Agreement

Scale With Michelle

Scale With Michelle will prepare all the documents to create a new non-profit organizing for public benefit, for a flat service fee of $697.00. Our standard turnaround time is 5-7 business days to prepare a completed draft that is ready to file with the IRS. For a 48 hour turn around an expedited service fee of $250 will be assessed. Clients are responsible for filing and publishing prepared articles of incorporation with associated filing fee as well as filing the prepared IRS exemption application (Form 1023) and related documents with associated IRS filing fee.

*Note*

Articles of Incorporation filing and publishing fees are typically $70-$500 based on State

The IRS filing fee is currently $600 if your organization expects to receive revenues of $50,000 or more per year. If your annual revenue is expected to be less than $10,000 the IRS filing fee is $275.

Our services include the following:

  • Conferencing with you to discuss the purpose and programs in detail;
  • Drafting the articles of incorporation to conform to 501 (c)(3) requirements and state law;
  • A Step-by-Step walk through to file and publish articles of incorporation with the state;
  • Obtaining a new EIN number for the new corporation;
  • Drafting the bylaws to reflect the desired governance structure;
  • Drafting organizational meeting minutes with required resolutions;
  • Drafting Conflict of Interest governance policy the IRS recommends;
  • Drafting Business Plan Overview;
  • Drafting the Revenue Projection Spreadsheet;
  • Drafting the IRS exemption application (Form 1023 and related statements);
  • Providing an explanatory letter regarding operational rules applicable to the new nonprofit;

For standard services a 50% deposit is due upon signing this service agreement, the remaining balance is due once completed drafts along with 1023 Form are ready to be submitted. For expedited services full payment is due upon signing this service agreement. By signing this document you are agreeing that you have fully read and understand the terms and conditions of the services being provided by Scale With Michelle.
